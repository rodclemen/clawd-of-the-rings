# What's new in Clawd of the Rings

The changes you'd notice in the Clawd of the Rings universe, newest first. The full, detailed history (every fix, every version) lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash).

## Clawd Clash 3.5.0 (2026-10-16)

- **A new universe.** Clawd of the Rings joins Star Clawds, Marvelous Clawds, Detective Clawds, Doctor Clawd, The Clawd Files and Stevie Clawdberg: pick it in **Settings → Universe**, or in the screen saver's Options.
- **75 characters from The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit**, each with a signature move. Gandclawd the Grey slamming his staff down (*"You shall not pass!"*), Frodough slipping the Ring on and fading to a ghost, Samwise and his potatoes, Leggolas surfing on a shield, Boromeme's *"One does not simply…"*, Éowin's *"I am no manager!"*, the elves, Rohan and Gondor, the Ents and Thorin's company. Facing them, Sourun and the Eye, Sarumanual and the palantír, the wraiths, the Ballrog, Smeagull arguing with himself, Shellob, the orcs and Uruks, Smaug and Azog. HEM turns up in Rohan's helmet, and it comes off to let the buns out.
- **22 places**, from Bag Endpoint and the Prancing Ponytail to Rivendev, Lothlorem, the Mines of Mori-ah, Helm Chart's Deep in the rain, Minas Tiered, Mount Doomscroll and Smaug's hoard in the Lonely Mount-point, each with something moving in it.
- **The Great Eagle** swoops in for Gandclawd, Frodough and Samwise when they're done; everyone else walks.
- **One character, never twice.** Gandclawd the Grey and the White, Arrogorn and Stride-r, Sarumanual and Sharkey, Sourun and the Necro-maintainer are never on screen together.
- **Smaug stays on his hoard.** Anywhere but the Lonely Mount-point, he's someone else.
- **Shellob's climb**: her thread shoots up to the top of your screen, she climbs out of sight and drops back down.
- **Nine transitions** from the books: the Eye, the map journey, the Red Book, the Great Eagles, the Ring, the beacons of Gondor, Gladriel's Mirror, Gandclawd's fireworks dragon and the palantír.
- **Arch-enemies face off first** (Gandclawd and the Ballrog, Frodough and Smeagull, Samwise and Shellob, Thorin and Azog, Bard and Smaug…).
- **Clawd Royale**: Clawd of the Rings' cast joins everyone else's in one scene when you pick Clawd Royale.
- **A trailer of its own**, at the top of this page.
